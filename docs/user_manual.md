# User Manual

## Getting Started
1. Launch the app: `streamlit run frontend/app.py`
2. Open http://localhost:8501 in your browser

## Pages
- **💬 Chat** — Ask health questions, get AI answers with source citations
- **📊 Health Metrics** — Log and track vitals (BP, HR, blood sugar, weight, etc.)
- **📁 Document Analysis** — Upload prescriptions or reports for AI analysis
- **📋 Chat History** — Review and search past conversations
- **⚙️ Settings** — Adjust model parameters, theme, and language
- **📖 About** — Project info and team credits

## Language Support
Select English, Hausa, or Swahili from the sidebar language selector.

## Disclaimer
AfriHealth Assistant provides general health information only.
It does not replace professional medical advice.
In an emergency, contact your nearest clinic or hospital.
